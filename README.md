# 🛢️ OilRefine — Enterprise Oil Refinery & Supply Chain Management

[![Java](https://img.shields.io/badge/Java-17%2B-orange.svg)](https://openjdk.org/)
[![Spring Boot](https://img.shields.io/badge/Spring%20Boot-3.x-brightgreen.svg)](https://spring.io/)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

> **Industrial inventory and refining stage management platform** built with Java Spring Boot for tracking crude oil batches, distillation processes, finished product quality testing, and dispatch logistics.

---

## 📌 Modules & Capabilities

* **Crude Oil Batch Tracking**: Monitor batch origin, sulfur density, and volume across distillation stages.
* **Quality Assurance Lab Tests**: Digital recording of Octane numbers, Viscosity, and Flash Point certifications.
* **Dispatch & Billing Ledger**: Automated tanker dispatch scheduling and commercial invoice generation.
